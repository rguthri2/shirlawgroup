Our Story

Collectively 50+ Years of Legal Expertise

At The Shir Law Group, P.A., helping you, the client, is the hallmark of our service and focus. Collectively, we bring over 50 years' experience and expertise to help you successfully navigate the legal landscape of today's complex personal and business matters.

It is our goal to assist you in making informed decisions prior to, during and after the litigation of your case. Many firms can litigate your case; we strive to give you both the personal attention you deserve, to sit with you at every step of the process and provide you with our guidance and expertise.

Our Firm is a leader in the community association field. We have the unique position of having represented all types of associations, as well as unit owners. While some firms put themselves out as representing associations, they do not understand nor appreciate the perspective of a unit owner driven litigation. The dual experience Shir Law Group has gained representing both sides helps us advise clients with a rare, balanced perspective.
