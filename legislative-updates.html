2018 Legislation Updates

A summary of Florida community association legislation and where to find the governing statutes, rules and regulatory resources.

Keeping Associations Informed

Each legislative session, the Florida Legislature considers and adopts changes affecting condominium associations, cooperatives, and homeowners associations. Shir Law Group monitors these developments and advises our association clients on how new laws affect governance, elections, financial reporting, records access, and dispute resolution.

In 2018, the Florida Legislature passed amendments affecting community association operations, including updates related to association recordkeeping, board member conflicts of interest, and dispute resolution procedures under Chapters 718 and 720, Florida Statutes. If your association has questions about how these changes affect your governing documents or day-to-day operations, our Community Association practice group is available to help.

Quick Reference

  • Florida Condominium Act — Chapter 718, F.S.
  • Florida Homeowners' Association Act — Chapter 720, F.S.
  • DBPR — Division of Condominiums, Timeshares & Mobile Homes
  • Florida Administrative Code — Chapter 61B
