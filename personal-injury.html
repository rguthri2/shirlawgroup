You can count on The Shir Law Group personal injury department to help you with your automobile accident and injury issues. We serve clients throughout Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Weston, North Miami, Sunrise, Boca Raton and Palm Beach.

Personal Injury Case Types

Automobile Accidents Animal Attacks Boating Accidents Faulty Products Work Accidents Motorcycle Accidents Property Claims Public Transportation Slip and Fall

If you have suffered personal injury due to an automobile accident, Shir Law Group will be there to assist you with the legal issues that arise. When you have been involved in an accident it is always a difficult time, and it is important to have an aggressive and caring legal advocate on your side. SLG's personal injury lawyers are experienced in the law regarding personal injuries, including those suffered at the hands of negligent medical personnel, so you can trust them with comprehensive legal representation.

Seek the Help of an Experienced Attorney

Car accidents occur frequently on Florida highways, and you can never be sure when you may be involved in one. Some insurance companies will try to give minimum compensation, or deny claims entirely by arguing their insured was not at fault. If you are represented by the SLG personal injury department, you will be in safe hands — we deal aggressively with insurance companies to ensure you receive the compensation you deserve.

Guy M. Shir is an experienced attorney who will work hard on your behalf to make sure that you are fairly compensated. Don't seek legal help anywhere else in South Florida except with The Shir Law Group personal injury department.
