Practice Area 03

Condominium & Homeowners Associations Representation & Litigation

A recognized leader in community association law, representing associations and individual unit owners alike.

Trusted Counsel for Condominiums, Cooperatives & HOAs

One principal area of concentration for Shir Law Group is Community Association Law. The Firm provides legal counseling for Condominium, Cooperative and Homeowners Associations. The Firm also provides counseling to Master Associations and owners' ad hoc committees.

The Shir Law Group assists its Association clients with drafting, amending and recording their governing documents, as well as negotiating and reviewing cable, satellite and construction contracts.

Our Firm is a leader in the community association field. We have the unique position of having represented all types of associations, as well as unit owners — a dual perspective that helps us advise clients on both sides of a dispute.

Services We Provide

  • Drafting, amending and recording governing documents
  • Negotiating and reviewing cable, satellite and construction contracts
  • Day-to-day counseling for officers and directors
  • Collection and foreclosure representation
  • Construction law matters
  • Covenant enforcement actions before the Division of Land Sales, Condominiums & Mobile Homes, Mediation and Circuit Court

Guy M. Shir is primarily responsible for providing legal counseling to officers and directors on the day-to-day operation of the Association, and manages all practice areas related to Collection/Foreclosure and Construction Law.
