Guy M. Shir, Esq.

Guy M. Shir is primarily responsible for providing legal counseling to officers and directors on the day-to-day operation of community associations. Mr. Shir also manages practice areas related to Collection & Foreclosure, Construction Law, and other legal needs of the Association.

He represents Condominium and Homeowner Associations, as well as individual unit owners, in covenant enforcement actions before the Division of Land Sales, Condominiums, Mobile Homes, the Homeowner division of Mediation, and Circuit Court.
